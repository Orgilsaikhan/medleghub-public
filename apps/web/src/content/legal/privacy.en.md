# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 September 2026\
**Last updated:** 30 September 2026

> This policy is published in Mongolian and English. If the two versions differ, the Mongolian version prevails.

## 1. Introduction

MedlegHub ("MedlegHub", "we", "us", "our") is an online programming education platform in the Mongolian language. Through the medleghub.com website, users can take courses and lessons, work on programming exercises, write and run code in their browser, and track their progress.

This Privacy Policy explains why and on what basis we collect, process and use your personal data, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. It applies to the medleghub.com website and all related services (the "Service").

We have drafted this policy with the aim of meeting the requirements of the Law of Mongolia on the Protection of Personal Data (Хүний хувийн мэдээлэл хамгаалах тухай хууль) and other applicable legislation.

Please read this policy carefully before using the Service. If you have questions, contact us using the details in section 16 "Contact".

## 3. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the information needed to provide the Service. Each category is described below.

### 3.1. Account information

When you register, we collect your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as an irreversible hash and are never stored in their original form. We also keep:

- your registration date and account role (learner or administrator);
- the date you accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

We do not ask for your exact age; when registering, you only confirm that you are 16 or older.

### 3.2. Profile information

You may upload a profile picture. This is optional. We resize and re-encode uploaded pictures and remove embedded metadata (for example, location or device information). We also store your settings, such as whether your profile is hidden from others and whether to show motivational messages.

### 3.3. Sign-in and email verification

To keep you signed in, we use a cookie containing a time-limited, secret sign-in token. When you change your password, we record when it was changed so that sessions on your other devices are signed out.

To verify your account, reset your password or change your email address, we send a one-time code to your email. Codes are sent and checked through our database provider's authentication service (Supabase Auth), so your email address is registered there. Codes expire after 10 minutes. We do not use third-party sign-in services (for example, signing in with Google or Facebook).

### 3.4. Course and progress information

The courses you have enrolled in, the lessons you have completed and when, the achievements (badges) you have earned, and statistics derived from these (for example, the number of completed lessons and exercises).

### 3.5. Source code and execution results

The source code you write when working on exercises, and the results of compiling and running it (output, error messages, test results). How we process this is described in section 6 "Code Submission and Execution".

### 3.6. Other user content

Messages you send through the "Feedback" form, their type (idea, bug, other) and the address of the page you were on when sending them.

Posts you publish in the Community section (title, content and the language and type tags you choose), your comments, and which posts you have "liked". We also keep when you accepted the Community guidelines and, if you break them, your suspension history (which suspension it was, the reason, its length and the administrator who issued it). When you report a post or comment with the "Report" button, the reason and details you give and a copy of the reported content are stored together with feedback.

### 3.7. Payment and subscription information

When you purchase a paid plan, we keep the payment amount, currency, plan length (in months), payment status, payment date, the payment provider's transaction reference and your plan's expiry date.

Payments are made on the payment provider's page (using a QR code or a banking app). We therefore do not receive or store your card number or your banking app sign-in details.

**Promo codes.** When you use a promo code, we keep which code you used, when, and the payment made with it or the free months it gave. We also keep the discount percentage and amount applied to each payment.

**Referral program.** We create and keep a referral code for every account. If you signed up with someone else's referral code, we keep who referred you and the dates when you:

- verified your email;
- used your referral discount;
- made your first successful payment.

We also keep the number of months given to the referrer as a reward.

### 3.8. Support communications

When you contact us by email, phone or the "Feedback" form, your name, contact details and the content of our communication.

### 3.9. IP address and technical information

Each time you access the website or our servers, technical information such as your IP address, browser and operating system type, the page requested, and the date and time is automatically recorded in our servers' logs and those of our hosting providers.

### 3.10. Cookies and browser storage

See section 7 "Cookies and Similar Technologies".

### 3.11. Security and abuse-prevention information

Request frequency (for example, the number of sign-in or code attempts in a short period), and load, error and timeout information from the code compilation and execution services.

We do not collect sensitive data (for example, information about health, religion or political opinions). Please do not include your own or other people's personal data in source code, feedback or other content.

## 6. Code Submission and Execution

When you work on programming exercises, your source code is processed as described below.

**Where code runs.** Depending on the programming language, your code runs either in your browser or on our servers:

- JavaScript, Python and algorithm (Flowgorithm) exercises run in your browser. Their source code is not sent to our servers to be executed.
- C and C++ code is sent to our compilation service, compiled to WebAssembly, and then run in your browser.
- SQL (MySQL) code is sent to our SQL execution service, run against a temporary database created for that request, and the result is returned to you. The temporary database is deleted after execution.

**Storage of source code.** Your source code is processed in server memory only while your session in the code editor is running. Once you leave the editor, your source code is not stored on our servers. To speed up repeated compilations, compiled results (not source code) may remain temporarily in server memory; they are removed automatically when that memory fills up or the server restarts. Drafts of algorithm exercises are saved in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device.

**Isolated environments.** The compilation and execution services on our servers run in environments (sandboxes) isolated from other systems and from other users' data.

**Resource limits.** We limit running time, memory, code size and the number of requests. If a limit is exceeded, execution may be stopped or the request rejected.

**Automated evaluation.** Whether you have completed an exercise is checked by automatically comparing your code's output against prepared tests. If the tests pass, the lesson is marked as completed and reflected in your progress and achievements.

**Security monitoring.** To protect the Service, we record use of the compilation and execution services in server logs (request time, programming language, type of result, time taken, errors). These logs do not contain your source code. If we detect misuse, we may take action under the Terms of Service.

We recommend that you do not put secrets such as passwords, API keys or personal data in your code.

## 7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Browser local storage is similar and keeps information only on your device.

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary for the Service to work, and browser storage that remembers your preferences.

| Name / type | Category | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `suralts_token` (cookie) | Strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in. Cannot be read by page scripts (httpOnly). | 7 days or until you sign out |
| `suralts_session` (cookie) | Strictly necessary | Tells the web page that you are signed in. Contains no secret value. | 7 days or until you sign out |
| Hosting/CDN security cookies | Strictly necessary | May be set by our hosting provider to protect against attacks and automated traffic. | Per the provider's policy |
| Browser local storage | Preferences | Remembers settings such as light or dark mode, lesson page layout, document language and the "Help" warning. | Until you delete it |
| Browser local storage | Functional | Remembers drafts of algorithm (Flowgorithm) exercises and the page to return to after payment. If you arrived through a referral link, remembers the referral code until you sign up. Remembers which announcements you have closed so they are not shown again. | Until you delete it. The referral code is deleted once you sign up |

We do not use analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If we start using such technologies, we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent in advance.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. However, if you block strictly necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in to the Service.

## 11. Data Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss and alteration. These include:

- communicating with the website and API over encrypted connections (HTTPS);
- storing passwords only as irreversible hashes;
- verifying email addresses with one-time codes;
- storing the sign-in token in a cookie that page scripts cannot read;
- limiting access to personal data to what staff need for their duties;
- isolating the code compilation and execution environments and applying resource limits;
- limiting the number of sign-in and code attempts and other requests;
- removing metadata from profile pictures.

No method of transmitting or storing data over the internet is 100% secure. While we cannot guarantee absolute security, we continually work to reduce risks. Please help protect your account by using a unique, strong password and never sharing your password or one-time codes with anyone.

## 12. Your Rights

Under the Law on the Protection of Personal Data, you have the following rights:

- **Right to be informed.** To know what data we process about you, why, on what basis and for how long.
- **Right of access.** To obtain a copy of the personal data we process about you.
- **Right to correction.** To have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
- **Right to deletion.** To have your personal data deleted, unless we are required by law to keep it.
- **Right to withdraw consent.** To withdraw your consent at any time (see section 5).
- **Right to information about disclosures and transfers.** To know to whom and on what basis your data has been disclosed, and whether it has been transferred abroad.
- **Right to complain.** If you believe your rights have been violated, to contact us first, and to apply to the National Human Rights Commission of Mongolia or a court.
- Other rights provided by law.

**Things you can do yourself.** In "Settings" you can:

- change your name, profile picture and password;
- change your email address (the new address is confirmed with a code);
- hide your profile from others;
- delete your account. Your account and its related data are permanently deleted exactly 2 hours after you make the request. Until then, you can cancel the request.

**Other requests.** Send other requests from your registered email address to info@medleghub.com, stating clearly which right you wish to exercise. To prevent your data being disclosed to someone else, we may ask for additional information to verify your identity. We will respond within the period required by law.

MedlegHub is an educational platform, so teenagers may use it.
